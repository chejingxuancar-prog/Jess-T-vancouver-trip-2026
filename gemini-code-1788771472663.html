PRACTICAL TIPS & SAFETY

Transit Payment: Simply tap contactless credit/debit cards or mobile wallets at all SkyTrain gates and bus readers. Single fares include 90 mins of free transfers.
Shellfish Limit: Maximum 12 oysters per person daily. Required tools: shucking knife, thick cut-resistant gloves, bucket, and thick-soled boots.
Tide Timing: Always explore Malaspina Galleries and tide pools within 1.5 hours before or after low tide.
